Privacy Policy

Last updated · March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JJ & RR Assessoria Técnica Comercial LTDA (hereafter "we", "the firm" or "JJ & RR Advisory") collects, uses and protects personal data provided by visitors of this editorial portal and by clients of our advisory services, in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados — Law No. 13.709/2018, LGPD).

1. Data controller

The data controller for the purposes of this policy is JJ & RR Assessoria Técnica Comercial LTDA, registered under CNPJ 03.529.509/0001-14, with office at R. Zacarias de Gois, 1296, Apt 32, Parque Colonial, São Paulo, SP, CEP 04.610-003. For any request related to the treatment of your personal data, you may contact us at contact@sotecidos.online.

2. What data we collect

We collect only the data strictly necessary to answer your contact and to deliver the advisory services you have requested. This typically includes:

  • Identification data: name, business name, position;
  • Contact data: e-mail address, phone number, city;
  • Business context you voluntarily share when requesting orientation (for example, size of the business, sector of activity, main challenges);
  • Navigation data automatically collected by our portal for basic security and performance purposes (IP address, browser type, pages visited).

3. Why we use your data

Your data is used strictly for the purposes below, and never for unrelated activities:

  • To answer your contact requests and general inquiries;
  • To deliver and manage the advisory services contracted with the firm;
  • To send you editorial and orientation content you have explicitly requested;
  • To comply with legal, regulatory and fiscal obligations that apply to our activity;
  • To protect the security and integrity of our portal.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases, as provided by Articles 7 and 11 of the LGPD: your consent, the performance of a contract or preliminary steps requested by you, compliance with a legal obligation, and the legitimate interest of the firm in operating and improving its advisory activity.

5. Sharing of data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. Personal data may be shared, in a restricted and controlled way, only in the following situations: with service providers who directly support our operation (for example, e-mail hosting) under confidentiality obligations; when required by a competent public authority; or when necessary to defend the rights of the firm in judicial or administrative proceedings.

6. Data retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above or to comply with legal retention periods. After that, data is deleted or anonymized following our internal data governance procedures.

7. Your rights as a data subject

Under the LGPD, you have the right to: confirm the existence of processing; access your data; correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data; request anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; request portability; request information on public and private entities with which we shared your data; and revoke consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Cookies

Our portal uses basic cookies for essential navigation and performance. We do not use advertising or behavioral tracking cookies on this portal. You can configure your browser to block or restrict cookies at any time.

9. Security

We adopt technical and administrative measures adequate to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or destruction. Access to personal data inside the firm is restricted to the team members who need it to perform their duties.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in our activity or in the applicable legislation. The current version and its last update date are always available on this page.
