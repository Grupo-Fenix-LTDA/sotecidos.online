About the firm

A management consultancy for the people who cut, sew and sell.

JJ & RR Assessoria Técnica Comercial LTDA has been active in São Paulo since November 1999. Our practice focuses on business management consulting for small textile enterprises: family-run ateliers, independent fabric shops, small confection workshops, artisanal producers and micro-enterprises operating in and around the traditional textile districts of São Paulo.

Our work is quiet and technical: we help owners organize their commercial routines, structure their pricing, understand their real margins, build simple stock controls, formalize supplier relationships and prepare their operations for growth. We do not sell finished goods, we do not operate a store and we do not resell fabrics. Our product is guidance.

The Sotecidos portal is our editorial and educational channel — a place where we publish orientation content, practical checklists and short explainers aimed at the small business owner who works with textiles every day.

What we do

Advisory services and educational content for textile micro-enterprises.

Our services are structured around the day-to-day operational needs of small textile businesses. Each engagement is delivered directly by our own team, from our São Paulo office, with no outsourcing and no third-party intermediation.

Commercial management consulting

Structured review of the client's commercial routines: sales flow, customer records, pricing policy, discount rules and cash discipline. Delivered as a diagnostic report with prioritized action items.

Pricing and margin orientation

Help owners understand the real cost of each meter, each piece, each production run — and set prices that reflect materials, labor, fixed costs and a defensible margin.

Stock and supplier organization

Practical guidance to structure basic stock controls, categorize suppliers, negotiate payment terms and reduce dead stock without over-engineering the system.

Formalization support

Orientation for micro-entrepreneurs on tax regime choice, invoice discipline, basic recordkeeping and the operational habits required to sustain formal operations over time.

Educational content (Sotecidos)

Short written orientations, checklists and practical explainers published on this portal and shared with our advisory clients. Editorial work maintained in-house by our own team.

Owner mentoring sessions

Recurring one-to-one sessions with the business owner to review indicators, discuss operational decisions and keep the improvement agenda moving between diagnostics.

How we work

Direct, in-house, and answerable to a single owner.

Every engagement is conducted directly by our internal team, from initial contact to final delivery. We do not operate as an affiliate network, we do not resell leads to third parties, we do not intermediate for other consultancies and we do not run distributed or franchised operations. All communications — inbound e-mails, phone calls, WhatsApp messages, meeting requests — are handled by the same team responsible for the advisory work itself.

Our editorial content on the Sotecidos portal is produced internally, published under our own responsibility and served identically to any visitor who accesses the site, regardless of device, region or time of access. The content of the portal, its purpose and its authorship are the same for every reader.
